How It Works

Here is how it works.

You bring the media to the booth, we quote it, we transfer it, and you pick it up when it is ready.

Where It Starts

Uncle John's Flea Market

Drop off and pick up at the booth in Cedar Lake, Indiana on Saturday or Sunday.

Typical turnaround: Most orders are completed in 1 to 3 weeks.

Steps

From drop-off to pickup

01

Bring your media to the booth

Bring tapes, reels, slides, or photographs on Saturday or Sunday to Uncle John's Flea Market in Cedar Lake.

02

Get a quote

Projects are quoted by tape or reel, with pricing based on running length and media type.

03

We transfer it

We transfer the media and clean up the audio and video when we can.

04

Pick up your finished order

Come back to the booth for pickup when your originals and finished copies are ready.

Before You Drop Off

Important things to know

No mailing

Your media stays local. You are not mailing it somewhere else.

Direct communication

If there is a problem or extra work is needed, we contact you before adding charges.

Older media has risk

As with any transfer service, old tapes and reels always carry a small chance of damage during conversion.

Most jobs take 1 to 3 weeks

Project timing depends on media type, length, and condition.
